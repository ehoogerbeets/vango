## Notes

- Usable roof length is about **160"**. The Webasto takes **44.1"** and stands above the panel plane; about **115.9"** remains for the main solar runway. Side gaps beside the shroud, outside the **4"** intake keep-out, carry the narrow N-type string ([electrical](../../02-architecture/electrical.md)).
- Panel tops flush with the rack and fairing. A **30 mm** frame under the Orion’s **5.4"** top puts the underside near **4.2"**, which clears the **4.1"** MaxxFan. A **35 mm** frame is tight. Heights: [climate](../../02-architecture/climate.md).
- Pick one rack family before ordering panels. The fairing height sets the panel top.
