## Summary

Calm modern cabin. Cabinet and drawer panels are **carbon skins over an end-grain balsa core**, faced with **maple veneer** so the room reads as wood. The tube frame behind those panels stays removable at the faces for plumbing and electrical service.

## Goals

- Maple-faced panels on balsa-core carbon for cabinet faces, drawer faces, drawer bottoms, and shelves
- Edges sealed, then banded in maple, so the core and the carbon edge stay hidden
- Match the calm, contemporary interior in [vision](../../00-overview/vision.md)

## Design notes

Panel stock is DragonPlate carbon / end-grain balsa, not Nomex. **1/4"** (overall about 0.31" with the 0.03" skins) for faces and drawer bottoms. **1/2"** balsa where a shelf span exceeds about 18" or the panel carries a heavy pan. Largest stock sheet is **24×48"**, with two free saw cuts. Catalog: [dragonplate.com/carbon-fiber-balsa-core](https://dragonplate.com/carbon-fiber-balsa-core).

DragonPlate rates balsa **poor for moisture** and the panel to **140°F**. Seal every cut edge before veneer or edge band. Keep these panels out of shower spray and out of a sun-baked ceiling. The textured face is the one that takes glue.

Maple is **10-mil paper-backed veneer** (~0.020"), bonded with epoxy, both sides of a door or drawer front. Peel-and-stick is a sample-only adhesive in a closed van.

Frame members stay the carbon tubes already sized for the bed and cabinets (1×1" storage, 2×1" where someone sits or the bed lands). Faces screw off. The bed platform itself stays an **open slat frame**, not a balsa deck.

| 2026-10-25 | Visible face: **maple veneer** | Reads as wood cabinetry | Exposed carbon weave; walnut, oak, bamboo | decided |
